---
description: Party mode — several specialists weigh in on one question, then a synthesis
argument-hint: <question or decision to discuss> [agents to include]
---

Run a multi-agent roundtable on: $ARGUMENTS

1. Pick the 3–5 agents whose perspective matters for this question (or the ones the user
   named). Say who and why in one line.
2. Round 1 — spawn them in parallel. Each gets the question, the relevant file paths, and
   the instruction: "Give your position from your role's perspective in under 150 words:
   recommendation, main reason, biggest risk. Read-only — change no files."
3. Round 2 — only if positions conflict: send each dissenting agent the opposing position
   and ask for a rebuttal or a concession, under 100 words.
4. Present to the user: each position in one or two lines, where they agree, where they
   disagree and why, and your recommended decision.
5. The user decides. If the decision changes an approved artifact, recommend `/bmad-change`.
